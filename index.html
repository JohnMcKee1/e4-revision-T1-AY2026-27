Quick revision notes

Vocabulary • Animals

Know the meanings and uses of words such as habitat, endangered, extinct, species, poacher, threat, protect, survive, cruel, welfare, cooperate, damage, disease, native and environment.

Examples:
A habitat is the natural home of a plant or animal.
An endangered species is at risk of becoming extinct.
A poacher hunts animals illegally.
Grammar • Reading & Writing

Comparatives

Use -er + than with many short adjectives (smaller than); more/less + adjective + than with many longer adjectives (more dangerous than). Remember big → bigger and heavy → heavier.

Combining sentences

Use and to join affirmative ideas; or in negative alternatives; both … and … to include two; neither … nor … to reject both.

Grammar • Listening & Speaking

Modals and advice

must / have to / need to = obligation; mustn't = prohibition; don't have to = no obligation; should / ought to = strong advice; might / could = possible or softer advice.

Contrasts and signposting

However, yet, even though, on the contrary show contrasts (but their sentence patterns differ). First, furthermore, finally, to sum up help organise an argument.
