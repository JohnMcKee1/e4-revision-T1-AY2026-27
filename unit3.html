Quick revision notes

Vocabulary · Transport

Review key Unit 3 terms: commuter, destination, traffic congestion, public transport, prevent, practical, outskirts, fuel, convenient, crash, fine, safety.

A commuter travels regularly to work or study. Traffic congestion means crowded, slow roads.
Reading & Writing grammar

Suggestions

should + base verb: You should cycle. It is important to + verb: It is important to wear a helmet. -ing as a subject: Cycling is a good idea.

First conditional and unless

If + present simple, will / won't be able to + verb: If the bus comes, we will be able to leave. Unless means if not: We will be late unless we leave.

Listening & Speaking grammar

Comparatives and superlatives

Fast → faster than → the fastest. Comfortable → more comfortable than → the most comfortable. Use much / a lot before comparatives, and by far with superlatives.

Recommendations

We ought to... / I'd suggest that... / I think it would be safer if...
