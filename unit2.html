Vocabulary • The Environment

Review these important Unit 2 words and expressions: absorb, atmosphere, annual, cause, consequence, ecosystem, fossil fuel, greenhouse gas, logging, predict, rainforest, affordable, alternative, benefit, disaster, risk, resource.

Examples:
Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere.
An ecosystem includes living things and their environment.
A consequence is a result of an action; a benefit is a positive result.
Grammar • Reading & Writing

Cause and effect

Cause → effect: Air pollution leads to health problems. Cutting down trees causes habitat loss. Rising temperatures result in changes to ecosystems.

Effect → cause: Habitat loss is caused by logging. Flooding is due to heavy rain.

Because vs because of

because + subject + verb: Plants died because it did not rain.
because of + noun phrase: Plants died because of the drought.

Giving examples

For example / for instance introduce an example. Such as / including / particularly / especially identify examples or emphasize items.

Language • Listening & Speaking

Transitions

Sequence: first of all, next, finally.
Contrast: however, but, on the other hand, in comparison.
Add: also, in addition, on top of that.
Summarise: overall, all in all, to sum up.

Advantages and disadvantages

Positive: A major benefit is cleaner air; the good thing about cycling is lower cost.
Negative: A drawback is the cost; a disadvantage is that it takes longer.
