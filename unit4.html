Quick revision notes

Vocabulary · Customs and Traditions

Review key Unit 4 terms: anniversary, ceremony, preserve, tradition, culture, generation, appearance, exchange, multicultural, die out, thoughtful.

To preserve a tradition is to keep it alive. A custom may die out if people stop practising it.
Reading & Writing grammar

Avoiding absolute statements

Use many, can, tend to instead of always saying all or never.

Frequency adverbs

usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never. Before the main verb: She often visits. After be: He is usually early.

Listening & Speaking grammar

Cause and effect

Because + clause; because of + noun phrase; that's why + result.

Present perfect

Have/has + past participle: She has visited. Have you ever...? for experience. For specific finished times (last Friday), use past simple.
